# Building with Intent

A practical take on making products that feel polished without over-designing.

## Why Intent Matters

When building software, every decision should have a purpose. This isn't about over-engineering — it's about being deliberate with your choices.

## Key Principles

1. **Start with the problem** — Understanding what you're solving before jumping to solutions
2. **Embrace constraints** — Limitations often lead to better, more focused designs
3. **Iterate with purpose** — Each iteration should move you closer to your goal

## Conclusion

Building with intent means making conscious decisions rather than defaulting to the obvious choice. It's the difference between a product that works and one that feels crafted.
